    It's Friday night. You've survived the week, your shoulders feel like concrete, and your brain is begging for something that isn't a spreadsheet or a group chat. You think: I need to get out of the house. A concert, maybe. A small festival. Or a hike, somewhere green, somewhere quiet.

    So you open Instagram. Then X. Then a WhatsApp group where someone forwarded a poster two weeks ago. Forty minutes later you've seen five posters, three of them from different years, one with a ticket link that looks suspicious, and you still don't know if anything is actually happening this month.

    If that sounds familiar, this article is for you. We want to show you how two tools on GoWithDME, GoWithDME Search and Dea, our AI assistant, now work together so that finding real events in Indonesia feels less like detective work and more like asking a friend who happens to know everything.

    The real problem: trusted event info in Indonesia is scattered

    Indonesia has no shortage of things to do. Ask around and everyone has a story: a concert in Bandung that sold out in a day, a festival in Jakarta that a friend swears changed their year, an exhibition nobody heard about until it was almost over, a hiking trip someone organized last minute.

    The hard part has never been a lack of events. The hard part is finding reliable information about them. Here's what usually goes wrong:

    • Information is scattered. One poster is on Instagram, the lineup is in a story that disappeared, and the ticket link is in a tweet from three weeks ago.
    • Old posts keep circulating. A poster from a past edition gets reposted and you only realize after you've planned your weekend around it.
    • It's hard to know what's legit. When you're about to spend real money, "is this the official info?" is a fair question.
    • You search alone. Even when you find the perfect event, your friends are busy, broke, or in another city, and you wonder who you could go with.

    That last point matters more than people admit. A lot of us skip good events not because we can't find them, but because going alone feels awkward. That's why GoWithDME has never been only a listing site. It's an info portal for concerts, festivals, exhibitions, and hiking in Indonesia, and it also helps people find travel companions.

    Two tools, one goal: help you find real events

    GoWithDME is run by Kalfin Agus Setiawan through Jurkon Studio (you can read more on the About page). The idea is simple: gather event info in one place, keep it curated, and make it easy to reach. Two tools now sit at the front of that idea.

    GoWithDME Search: your fast lane to the listings

    GoWithDME Search is the straightforward one. You go there, type what you're looking for, and look through what's listed. It's built for the moment when you already know what you want: a certain type of event, a certain artist, a certain kind of trip.

    Think of it as the search bar for everything on GoWithDME. If you like browsing and comparing on your own, this is where you'll feel at home. The full collection also lives on the main Event page, where you can scroll through what's currently listed.

    Dea: the friend who answers when you don't know what to search

    Not every Friday night starts with a clear plan. Sometimes you don't know what you want. You only know you feel off. That's where Dea comes in.

    Dea is GoWithDME's AI assistant, and she works in two modes:

    1. Companion mode. This is for chatting and venting. Had a rough day? Feeling burnt out? You can just talk. She isn't there to push tickets at you.
    2. Event info mode. When you ask about events, Dea answers using only curated GoWithDME data. She doesn't pull random stuff from the internet and she doesn't guess.

    That second point is the whole reason we're comfortable putting an AI in front of people who are about to make plans and spend money.

    How Search and Dea work together in real life

    Here's the part we're most happy about. These two tools aren't competing. They cover each other's blind spots.

    Search is great when you know the keyword. Dea is great when you only know the feeling. And when Dea can't find a match in the curated data, she doesn't make something up to keep the conversation going. She sends you to the Event page or to GoWithDME Search so you can look for yourself.

    That small rule changes everything. An AI that says "I couldn't find that, here's where to look" is far more useful than one that confidently invents a festival that doesn't exist. Nobody wants to book a trip to an event that was never real.

    Here's roughly how a conversation can feel. This is an illustration of the style, not a real log, and the event name is a placeholder.

    You: Dea, I'm so tired. Work has been a lot and I feel like I haven't done anything fun in months.

    Notice what happened. Dea started with you, not with a ticket. She offered an event only from real data. And when she had no answer for Bandung, she said so and pointed you to the right place. That's the behavior we care about most.

    What Dea can do, and what she can't

    It helps to be clear about what to expect, so here it is without the sales gloss.

    What Dea can do

    • Chat with you in companion mode when you need to vent or just talk through a heavy week.
    • Answer questions about events using curated GoWithDME data.
    • Help you narrow things down when you only have a vague idea ("something chill", "something outdoors", "something with music").
    • Send you to the Event page or to Search when she can't find what you asked for.

    What Dea cannot do

    • Invent events. If it isn't in the curated data, she won't pretend it is. This is a hard rule, not a preference.
    • Replace your own final check. Before you commit to anything, look at the listing itself on the Event page.
    • Be a professional counselor. Companion mode is a friendly place to vent. It isn't therapy, and if you're going through something serious, please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional.

    We'd rather be upfront about these limits than overpromise. You'll trust a tool more when it's honest about where it stops.

    Real-life scenarios where this helps

    Let's walk through a few situations to make this less abstract.

    "I want a concert this month and I live near Bandung"

    If you're after concerts in Bandung, start with GoWithDME Search and look through what's listed. If you'd rather talk it through, ask Dea. She'll look in the curated data, and if she can't find a match, she'll point you to the Event page instead of guessing. Either way you end up looking at real listings.

    "I'm visiting Jakarta and want to catch a festival"

    Festivals in Jakarta come in many flavors, and when you're traveling you don't have time to sift through old posters. Tell Dea what kind of vibe you like, or type your keywords into Search. If something fits, you'll see it. If it doesn't, you'll know quickly and can adjust your plan.

    "I want to hike but I don't want to go alone"

    This is where GoWithDME's other side matters. The portal covers hiking, and it also supports people who want to find a travel companion in Indonesia. If you've been putting off a trip because nobody's free, that's worth exploring on the portal. Start from the Event page to see what's listed, then think about who you'd like to go with.

    "I just want to see an exhibition, nothing loud"

    Not every plan needs a crowd and a stage. Exhibitions are on the portal too. Tell Dea you want something quiet and she can work with that in event info mode. For a specific example, here's where a real listing would go: [INSERT REAL EVENT FROM https://gowithdme.com/id/event].

    Tips to get better results from both tools

    • Be a little specific with Dea. "Something outdoors and relaxed" gives her more to work with than "anything".
    • Use Search when you know your keyword. An artist, a type of event, or a city can get you to the point quickly.
    • If Dea says she can't find it, take that seriously. It usually means it isn't in the curated data. Go to the Event page or Search and look yourself.
    • Check the listing before you commit. Use the info on the Event page as your starting point and double-check details before you plan travel or buy anything.
    • Vent first if you need to. If your head is too noisy to choose, use companion mode. Picking an event is easier after you've said the tired thing out loud.
    • Invite someone. If you find something you love, think about who might want to come. Events are better shared.

    GoWithDME started from a simple frustration: trusted event info in Indonesia shouldn't take an hour of tab-hopping to find. Kalfin Agus Setiawan and Jurkon Studio built the portal to put concerts, festivals, exhibitions, and hiking in one place, and to help people who want company on those adventures.

    Adding Dea was about meeting people where they actually are. Sometimes you open the site with a plan. Sometimes you open it at 11 PM, exhausted, with no idea what you want. We wanted both moments to feel welcome. And we wanted Dea to be the kind of assistant we'd trust ourselves: warm when you need to talk, careful when you need facts, and honest when she doesn't know.

    She won't invent one. She'll tell you she couldn't find it and send you to the Event page or to GoWithDME Search so you can check the listings yourself.
