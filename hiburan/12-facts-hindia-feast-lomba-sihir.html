12 Facts About Hindia, .Feast and Lomba Sihir (Updated November 2026)

    If you have ever shuffled an Indonesian indie playlist, the same voice has probably turned up under three different names. Hindia, .Feast and Lomba Sihir are separate projects, yet all three revolve around Baskara Putra, and all three have been in the news over the past year. This guide goes through twelve facts, and for each one it tells the story behind the date or the number. We rechecked the details against news reports and reference pages on 26 November 2026, so everything here reflects where things stand today.

    The big picture: one writer, three very different projects

    .Feast is a rock band from Jakarta that formed in 2012. Hindia is Baskara's solo project, started in 2018. Lomba Sihir began in 2019 as Hindia's live band and grew into a band of its own. Records from all three have come out on the label Sun Eater.

    Hindia is the personal one. Profiles describe the solo work as intimate, about life, relationships and mental health. .Feast is the loud one. The band built its name on rock songs about social and political frustration. Lomba Sihir is the collective one. Its debut was about the city of Jakarta, not about its frontman.

    Look at the release history, though, and the three seem to be drifting toward the same place. The rock band turned more personal in 2024, and the collective followed with a second album in 2025 and a deluxe edition in 2026. That is our reading of the sources, not something the artists have said in those words. The twelve facts below show how it happened.

    1. Hindia was planted in late 2018, after years of building .Feast

    Baskara had spent years pushing .Feast toward a national audience when, in late 2018, he started the solo project that became Hindia, according to the artist biography on Soundcharts. His first release under the name was the single "No One Will Find Me", which appeared on the compilation Bertamu.

    A Last.fm biography adds two details. He had been producing his own tracks as early as 2015, and he first treated Hindia as his main venture, with .Feast as a passion project and an outlet for his rock side. That split explains why the two projects sound so different even though the same person is singing.

    2. Two albums, then a mixtape nobody saw coming

    Hindia's catalogue is small and deliberate. The debut album Menari dengan Bayangan arrived on 29 November 2019, and the second album, Lagipula Hidup Akan Berakhir, followed on 21 July 2023. Then, on 24 February 2025, came the mixtape Doves, '25 on Blank Canvas, dropped with no pre-release campaign. The Jakarta Post reported that it passed a million plays on Spotify in under 48 hours.

    Baskara told the paper that the 16 tracks were recorded between September and January, and that the mixtape was a spontaneous reaction to events in Indonesia and to personal turmoil. The format gave him freedom, because a surprise drop needed no marketing build-up. He described the whole project as an empathy test. The title has a personal side too: in his religion, he explained, doves stand for letting go of grief and for good news received and delivered, and the loss of his cats inspired the name.

    On 7 June 2025 he staged a Jakarta showcase for the project. Akurat reported that he broke into tears on stage while friends rushed over to hug him.

    3. The 2019 debut is still the heavyweight

    Seven years on, Menari dengan Bayangan is still the biggest record in the Hindia catalogue. RRI reported that it passed 2.5 billion Spotify streams, which it described as the best performance of any of his albums. Songs from it such as "Evaluasi" and "Secukupnya" still sit high on his Spotify page today, with roughly 600 million and 506 million plays.

    The album has also become a subject of study. A paper from Universitas Islam Indonesia reads the record as a portrait of personal identity. Lomba Sihir's members, who had been playing with Baskara live, also worked on it, which is the link to fact 9.

    The next chapter may be on screen. Media Indonesia reported that Palari Films is adapting the album into a feature film of the same name, directed by Edwin, as part of seven new projects for the studio's tenth anniversary. We could not find a confirmed release date, so treat the film as announced, not scheduled.

    4. A big run of awards in seven months

    At the AMI Awards on 19 November 2025, Baskara took home five trophies, the most of the night. Four came as Hindia: Best of the Best Album and Best Alternative Album for the Doves mixtape, plus Best Alternative Solo Artist and Best Music Video for "everything u are". The fifth came with .Feast (fact 7).

    Popmama reported that he was stunned, and that Best Album was a long-held dream. He credited his team and his listeners, and JawaPos quoted his speech describing the record as the story of a mother who lost her child in 1998 and is still seeking justice.

    Seven months later, on 13 June 2026, Hindia received the Special Award for Indonesian Popular Music at Music Awards Japan 2026 in Tokyo, again for "everything u are". The previous winner of that category was Salma Salsabil, in 2025. IDN Times reported that he was the first Indonesian musician to perform on that awards stage, and he had played a Shibuya show two days earlier as part of the awards week. Spotify Japan reported that plays of the song jumped 142% after the ceremony.

    5. .Feast: from a 2012 rock band to a ten-year tour

    .Feast formed in Jakarta in 2012. The current lineup is Baskara Putra on vocals and synthesizer, Adnan S.P. and Dicky Renanda on guitar, and F. Fikriawan on bass. Drummer Adrianus Aristo Haryo, known as Bodat, played with the band from 2012 until he left in April 2023.

    The early years were busy. The first album, Multiverses, came out in 2017 with guest musicians on many tracks. In 2019 the band recorded "Gelora" with The Panturas for the SEA Games in the Philippines, and released "Tarian Penghancur Raya", a song about environmental and cultural threats that still has about 47 million Spotify plays today. In 2022 came the mini album Abdi Lara Insani, which a university thesis says arrived in April with eight tracks and was reissued in August as a complete edition with eleven. ANTARA then covered a tour called Tur Multisemesta in 2023 that marked ten years of the band.

    6. The third album almost didn't happen

    By 2024 .Feast was ten years in and tired. Malay Mail reports that the members believed Membangun & Menghancurkan might be their last album, after nearly breaking up beforehand.

    The comeback was built carefully. The singles came first, including "Konsekuens" and "Politrik", which ANTARA described as criticism wrapped in comedy and MerahPutih called aggressive and anthemic. Then, on 30 August 2024, the 15-song album arrived. Medcom reported that 12 producers worked on it, including Laleilmanino, Lafa Pratomo and Iga Massardi, with "Arteri" as the lead track.

    Adnan explained the goal: the band, long known for anger at the system, wanted listeners to know it in a more intimate way. A week before release, ANTARA ran a video headline welcoming "the new .Feast". The numbers suggest listeners agreed. "Nina" now has about 634 million Spotify plays and its lyric video about 155 million YouTube views, and "Tarot" has about 459 million Spotify plays.

    7. The record won Best Rock Album and closed with a finale in Kuala Lumpur

    Membangun & Menghancurkan won Best Rock Album at the AMI Awards 2025, the .Feast trophy in Baskara's five-trophy night. The band toured it as a full-album show called Pertunjukan Membangun & Menghancurkan. JawaPos reported that at the Surabaya concert in September 2025 the band paid tribute to ten people who died in the August 2025 unrest.

    The tour ended abroad. Reports say the final show was a two-night run at Zepp Kuala Lumpur, finishing on 6 June 2026, and Malay Mail noted that traditional dancers were a regular part of the production. For a band that once expected this album to be its last, closing the run in a venue like that is quite an ending.

    8. A vinyl edition is rolling out this month

    The story did not stop there. ANTARA reported on 17 November 2026 that the album is getting a vinyl release with Bojakrama Press, a label and publisher from Surabaya, distributed in stages through November. Copies go out through a charity auction, a draw that gives fans early access, and general sale.

    The packaging is elaborate: a 3D gatefold illustration, interactive packaging, a booklet with lyrics, credits and essays, plus a poster and stickers. The burning house from the original cover becomes a frame holding four swappable cards, so owners can choose how far the fire has spread. Baskara said the look aims to capture a hectic, overstimulated feeling, and that the album, with ten years of stories inside, feels like a monument. Bojakrama's representative said the label picks records for two reasons: personal taste and relevance to the spirit of the times. Dicky Renanda said the response to the album went well beyond what the band expected.

    9. Lomba Sihir began as a backing band, and the name has a story

    In an interview with The Jakarta Post, Baskara said Lomba Sihir formed in mid-2019 as the backing band for his Hindia material. He found it jarring at times to have the spotlight all to himself, and he wanted everyone to get the recognition they deserved, because the songs were always played together as a band. The group debuted on stage as Hindia's live band at We The Fest 2019.

    The name comes from Baskara too. Guitarist Rayhan Noor explained that he had formed a band called Lembaga Lomba Sihir back in college. The group liked the idea that music works like magic, but worried that the word "sihir" on its own sounded too dark, since it also means witchcraft, so they settled on Lomba Sihir, a magic contest. Keyboardist Tristan Juliano added that on stage everyone is racing to do some magic for the audience.

    10. Six members became five, and each has other bands

    Lomba Sihir is now Baskara Putra and Natasha Udu on vocals, Rayhan Noor on guitar and vocals, Tristan Juliano on keyboards and vocals, and Enrico Octaviano on drums. Bassist Wisnu Ikhsantama was in the band from 2019 to 2022, so the debut album was made by six people.

    Those six were already busy elsewhere. NME noted that Rayhan and Enrico play together in Martials, Rayhan and Wisnu in Glaskaca, and Tristan in Mantra Vutura, while Natasha releases her own music. The band told the press that it never tried to merge six identities into one, and a member told NME that this is why the album stays free from being tied to one or two genres.

    11. The debut album was a love letter to Jakarta, released fast

    The rollout was quick. The first single, "Hati dan Paru-Paru", arrived in February 2021 with a warm music video. The second, "Apa Ada Asmara", came on 12 March 2021, and NME described it as a disco-driven story about a woman looking for love in Jakarta. On 26 March 2021 the full album Selamat Datang di Ujung Dunia followed, only about a month after the first single.

    NME described the sound as a mix of quasi-funk, synth-pop and dance-infused soft rock, and the theme as Jakarta in all its chaos. The Jakarta Post called the band the latest "it" band of the indie scene. In December 2021 the band released the 1980s-flavored single "Pesona" for a Sun Eater compilation, and it was nominated for Best Band in Asia at the NME Awards 2022. In June 2023 it started its first tour, Parade Sihir, in four cities.

    12. A second album, and a deluxe edition this September

    The second album, Obrolan Jam 3 Pagi, came out on 7 May 2025, four years after the first. It has 16 tracks: an intro, two interludes, five songs released earlier and eight new ones. Popbela reports that the members wrote and produced everything themselves, that the sound is more organic and firmer than the debut, and that the band says the change was not planned. The closing track, named after the band, runs 4 minutes and 3 seconds and sums up almost five years together.

    Then, on 4 September 2026, came a deluxe edition, Setelah: Obrolan Jam 3 Pagi, with five new songs: "Vas Bunga", "Cerita Cinta", "Hampir", "Di Seberang" and "Sedikit Demi Sedikit". Natasha Udu explained that the members sat down one day and asked what came next, and agreed there was more, and more personal, to say. "Di Seberang", about loss and acceptance, was chosen as the first single because it set the tone for the rest. Enrico stressed that these five songs belong to the second album and are not a separate release.

    Latest: a duet with Banda Neira on someone else's record

    The newest Hindia news is a guest appearance. Producer Lafa Pratomo spent 12 years behind other artists' hits, and with the creative company KithLabo he has now released his first EP under his own name, Garis Tegak. It puts 12 musicians into six crossover tracks, released one at a time, and it was introduced at a press event in Kemang, South Jakarta, on 30 July 2026, according to Medcom. Among the pairings are Ari Lesmana with White Chorus, and Isyana Sarasvati with Jason Ranti on "Dogs Are Always True", the fifth track.

    The sixth track, "Sampaikan ke Kawan", is the one that ends the EP, and it teams Hindia with Banda Neira. It is announced for 1 December 2026, and our earlier report has the announcement details. The story of how the pairing came about is the interesting part. Lafa told Medcom that he had worked with Hindia before but never with Banda Neira, and wondered why nobody had put them together. Era.id reported that while discussing the idea, Baskara realized he had grown tired of writing songs about change and perseverance, and admitted to feeling frustrated and unsure how to write. Banda Neira's Ananda Badudu said that looking after yourself should come before taking on bigger, more complicated problems.

    What else Baskara has been up to in 2026

    • Collaborations: a single with Idgitaf called "Masih Ada Cahaya" (May 2026), and "Hari Ini", a song he co-wrote with Isyana Sarasvati.
    • Film soundtrack: Hindia appears on the soundtrack list of the film Tunggu Aku Sukses Nanti, alongside RAN.
    • Festivals: Rasvara Festival 2026, the first day of KLBB 2026, and a joint Hindia and .Feast appearance at Sunset di Kebun on 20 June 2026. He was also announced for Forestra 2026 with the Erwin Gutawa Orchestra.

    Bonus: the streaming numbers on 26 November 2026

    These are the figures we saw in the Spotify and YouTube apps today. They change daily, so read them as a snapshot.

    • Hindia on Spotify: "Rumah Ke Rumah" about 814 million plays, "Evaluasi" about 600 million, "Cincin" about 571 million, "everything u are" about 535 million.
    • .Feast on Spotify: "Nina" about 634 million, "Tarot" about 459 million, "o,Tuan" about 271 million.
    • Lomba Sihir on Spotify: "Ribuan Memori" about 77 million, "Mungkin Takut Perubahan" about 64 million, "Semua Orang Pernah Sakit Hati" about 38 million.
    • On YouTube: Hindia's "Rumah Ke Rumah" lyric video is at about 185 million views, .Feast's "Nina" lyric video at about 155 million, and the Lomba Sihir "Ribuan Memori" lyric video at about 24 million.

    One thing to notice: Spotify's "Popular" list is not sorted by lifetime plays. On Hindia's page, "everything u are" is listed first even though "Rumah Ke Rumah" has more total plays, which suggests the list leans on recent listening.

    Common questions

    Are Hindia, .Feast and Lomba Sihir the same band?

    No. They are three separate projects with different sounds. Baskara Putra sings in all three, and each one has its own releases and its own shows. .Feast and Lomba Sihir are bands with their own members, while Hindia is Baskara's solo name.

    Where should a new listener start?

    The numbers above are a simple guide: try each project's most-streamed songs first, then move on to the albums mentioned in this article. If you like the stories behind records, the 2025 mixtape Doves and the 2024 album Membangun & Menghancurkan both have plenty.
